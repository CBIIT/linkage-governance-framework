# A new approach to governance for complex data linkage

The Linkage Governance Framework’s mission is to enable complex linkages while upholding consent and protecting privacy and security. If successful, the framework would lead to more research studies linking data being initiated, linkages of multiple datasets happening faster, and complex linkages being completed for less costs. This could speed the transformation from data, to research, to actionable evidence. 

The framework proposes three advances:

1. **[Common Governance Process](/framework/common-governance-process.html)**: a new approach to support requests for complex linkage of individual-level health data. The process streamlines investigators’ efforts to request complex linkages, clarifies applicable governance rules, and generates decisions sooner. 
2. **Coordinating Body**: a governance support structure that gathers information from investigators, coordinates dataset holders reviews, and helps resolve governance rule conflicts that could block innovative research.
3. **Linkage Knowledge Library**: a means to collect information for future research about proposed linkages and the governance that has successfully created linked data.

## Elements of the Framework

The framework has five connected elements that support a new approach for complex linkages of individual-level health data. This framework was created for investigators pursuing complex linkages, but its use is voluntary. It may not be helpful for all linkages. Linkages that are straightforward (for example, one-to-one linkage) should use existing governance processes.  [Read a text description of framework elements](framework-elements.html){ .usa-sr-only}
